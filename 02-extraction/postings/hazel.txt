About Hazel

Hazel is the AI coworker for consumer brands. We connect to a brand's live data — Shopify, Klaviyo, Amazon, Meta, and dozens of others — and turn it into a teammate operators talk to every day. Ask "why did repeat purchase rate drop last week?" and 10 seconds later you get an answer, cited from live data.

Hazel also takes action herself, proactively flagging issues and making changes directly in Shopify and the other systems she connects to, on the way to running full playbooks autonomously.

We've raised more than $2M, are rapidly scaling, and serve some of the biggest consumer brands including Bogg Bag, Ultra, and OneSkin. You'll join a small, senior engineering team working directly with the founders.

What You'll Work On

In your first 60 days, you'll be extending Hazel's action layer — the writes back into the systems she connects to. Think: creating product bundles in Shopify, launching A/B tests in Klaviyo, and the human-in-the-loop scaffolding that makes all of it safe.

Beyond that:

Full-stack product features across TypeScript/React + Go services
AI agent workflows: streaming LLM responses, multi-step tool use, approvals
Integrations across Shopify, Klaviyo, Meta, Amazon, and more, both reading and writing

You'll work directly with the founders and customers. Code you ship today is in front of operators tomorrow.
